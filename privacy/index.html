Plain-language policy · 27 August 2026

How your data stays private

Your word pairs, notes, practice history, and recordings stay in this browser. We do not receive them.

What is stored

The app stores your confusion pairs, cues, mnemonics, recordings, results, and due dates on this device.

A purchased license token and its last verification result are stored in this browser.

What leaves your device

Ordinary logging and practice send no personal data.

Buying or restoring Pro sends the license token to Sociobot’s billing API for verification.

Sociobot/Dodo processes payment on its hosted checkout. This app never sees card details.

Microphone access

Microphone access is requested only after you press a record button.

Audio is captured locally. It is not speech-scored or uploaded.

Removing a recording or deleting its pair removes that audio from the app database.

Your control

Use JSON backup to move or preserve all pair data, including recordings.

CSV export contains resolved pair text and progress, but not audio.

Clearing this site’s storage deletes local data. A JSON backup can restore it.

Analytics and contact

There are no analytics, advertising cookies, trackers, or third-party runtime scripts. Questions may be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
